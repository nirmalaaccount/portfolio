01 Mandap.com · Self-initiated

The honest part

What these case studies don't claim

All three are design-stage revamps of live products — not 0→1 concepts. None are backed by user interviews, A/B tests or analytics access. Each case study says this upfront and names the exact validation I'd do next if I had that access.

Mandap — I measured the live DOM before touching Figma: 412 links on one page, 292 in the footer, a 3.35:1 contrast fail. I then re-ran the same script against my redesign and found 6 WCAG failures I had introduced. All six fixed and documented.

Elite Matrimony — The live page had 9 price points, 5 autoplay videos and 13 truncated testimonials undercutting the exclusivity it sells. I resequenced around the buyer's real decision path. No A/B data — I've named what I'd measure first.

ManyJobs — I checked the live recruiter site directly. Match scoring, bilingual posting, locality filtering — all already live. The revamp replaces benefit claims with working previews. No recruiter interviews; the case study is scoped accordingly.
