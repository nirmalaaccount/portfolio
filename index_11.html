Three live products. Each case study shows the audit, the decisions that came out of it, and — plainly — what I could not validate.

Mandap.com — redesigned homepage (Figma, 1512px)

01 Mandap.com · Self-initiated

Measuring before redesigning: an audit-led rebuild of a live wedding-venue homepage

I pulled real DOM counts and ran an automated contrast script against the live site — surfacing 412 links on one page, 292 stacked in the footer, and a 3.35:1 contrast failure. Then I ran root-cause analysis before designing, and re-audited my own redesign, which turned up six accessibility failures I had introduced myself.

DOM auditing WCAG contrast testing Problem definition IA at scale

Elite Matrimony — redesigned landing page (Figma, 1336px)

02 Elite Matrimony · In-house

Designing for the client who will never fill in a form casually

A private, invitation-only matchmaking service was using mass-market conversion tactics — nine price points, a five-video carousel, thirteen truncated testimonials — that undercut the exclusivity it sells. I resequenced the page around the buyer's real decision path: qualification, then trust, then price.

Heuristic audit Information architecture Content strategy Trust design

ManyJobs — recruiter landing page revamp (Figma, 1440px)

03 ManyJobs · In-house

From telling recruiters what the product does, to showing them

A live recruitment platform for entry-level Sales, Service and Manufacturing hiring in Tamil Nadu communicated its core value through descriptive benefit cards. I built a five-part component system and replaced the claims with working previews: a real ranked-applicant dashboard, and a side-by-side bilingual field demo.
